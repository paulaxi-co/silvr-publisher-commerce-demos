## Evidence and limitations

- Official publisher pages and editorial imagery were used for context and asset sourcing; see image `sourceUrl` entries in `src/publisherCatalog.ts` and `src/PublisherPages.tsx`.
- The Story + Rain “Reading Nooks to Revel In” hero and room photographs use photos from that article; the wicker chair cutout is one of the objects in the same publisher shopping edit: [source feature](https://storyandrain.com/article/decor-entertaining-inspired-home-reading-nooks-to-revel-in).
- The Gazette travel-valet tray asset is hosted by the publisher's Fort Belvedere store and is associated with its travel-tray collection.
- Each shoppable home image/card has its own media ID and hotspot record; the repeated Hayes cover photograph is assigned separate IDs when its crop differs. Product identifications are visual alternatives, not recognition results or exact SKU matches.
- There is no API endpoint or credential in the checkout. The fixtures and hotspot placement are manually curated for this demo; they do not demonstrate live visual recognition or inventory.
- No video asset with a verified product match and time association was found, so video remains editorial only.
